# User Journey Maps — Week 2

## Scenario A: "Has a coach-given plan, feels sick, needs to reschedule"
*(Mode: "I have my own plan")*

| Stage | User Action | Touchpoint | Thoughts/Feelings | Opportunity |
|-------|-------------|------------|--------------------|--------------|
| Trigger | Wakes up feeling unwell | — | 😟 Worried — "what about today's run?" | — |
| Discover | Opens the app, checks today's schedule | Home/Dashboard | 😐 Neutral, just checking | Show next run clearly on Home |
| Action | Taps "Can't train today?" | Reschedule/Adjust | 🤔 Slightly unsure — "will the app actually understand my situation?" | Use empathetic language, not robotic |
| Decision | Selects reason "Sick/Unwell" | Reschedule/Adjust | 😌 Relieved a relevant option exists | — |
| Response | App shows several options (not one forced suggestion) | Popup — Adjustment Suggestions | 🙂 Feels in control, not dictated to | Show brief data behind each suggestion |
| Resolution | Picks "Reschedule Manually" to stay aligned with the coach's plan | Popup → Schedule Detail | 😊 Satisfied, stays consistent with the coach's program | Keep the manual option always visible, not just auto-suggest |

**Insight:** The "Reschedule Manually" link (the small link under the Apply button in the wireframe) turns out to be critical for users in "I have my own plan" mode — not just a minor fallback.

## Scenario B: "Doesn't want to plan alone, wants the app to suggest a menu"
*(Mode: "Suggest for me")*

| Stage | User Action | Touchpoint | Thoughts/Feelings | Opportunity |
|-------|-------------|------------|--------------------|--------------|
| Trigger | No training plan set for the week | — | 😑 Doesn't feel like planning it themselves | — |
| Discover | Opens Training Calendar, sees it's empty | Training Calendar | 🤷 "What should I even train this week?" | Clear CTA: "Suggest a plan for me" |
| Action | Taps suggest/generate plan | Training Calendar → Short onboarding | 🙂 Curious | Ask a few quick questions: level, target race, available days |
| Response | App generates a weekly plan | Training Calendar (filled in) | 😊 Relieved, doesn't have to start from scratch | Still allow manual edits per day |
| Resolution | Adjusts 1–2 days to fit their schedule | Schedule Detail | 😌 Still feels in control | — |

---

## Key Takeaway

Both scenarios confirm the same core need identified in Week 1 research — flexibility and user control — but expressed differently depending on the mode:
- In **Mode A**, control means the ability to override the app and stick to an existing plan.
- In **Mode B**, control means the ability to edit an app-generated plan rather than accepting it as-is.

This reinforces that the Reschedule/Adjust feature must always offer a manual override path, regardless of which mode the user is in.
